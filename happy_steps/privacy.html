Privacy & safety

Private by physics. Careful by design.

A floor sensor cannot see a face or hear a word. On top of that, Happy Steps keeps the signal at home and sends only what is needed to care.

見ない、聞かない、送りすぎない。

Nothing to see or hear

A geophone measures floor motion only. There is no image, no audio, and no way to rebuild speech from it.

The signal stays home

All signal processing runs on the device in the home. Raw vibration is never uploaded.

Only small numbers leave

About 300 bytes per walk: time, steps, pace, steadiness, and “resident” or “unknown”. No names in the event.

WHAT GEMINI SEES

Exactly this, and nothing more.

  • Today’s walk events, in the open footstep-event/v1 format
  • A 14-day summary of the resident’s own usual days
  • How the family calls her (for example “お母さん”), chosen by the family
  • A system instruction that forbids diagnosis and asks for kind, simple words

SAFETY PRINCIPLES

Care that knows its limits.

Consent first

The person living in the home agrees to the sensor, can pause it, and can ask for it to be removed at any time. The family sees summaries, not a live feed.

No diagnosis

Happy Steps describes changes (“slower than usual”) and suggests human actions (“give her a call”). It never names a disease or gives medical advice.

A human decides

Alerts ask a person to check. Nothing happens automatically to the resident. Emergency services are a human choice.

Kind words, always

Messages spoken to the resident are short and cheerful. Worry goes to the family, not to her.

About the live demo: if you paste a Gemini API key, it stays in your browser and is sent only to Google’s Gemini API. The page has no server of its own. The demo uses lab recordings from consenting adult participants, with no names attached.
