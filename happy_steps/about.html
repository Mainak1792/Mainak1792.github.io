About

I live far from my own family. So I know the worry.

Happy Steps began as a PhD on reading people from floor vibrations. Moving to Tokyo, I saw how many parents live alone, and how much their families want a gentle way to know they are okay.

FOUNDER

Mainak Chakraborty

Researcher in structural vibration sensing and human-centred AI. PhD in Cyber-Physical Systems from IIT Delhi as a Prime Minister’s Research Fellow, now based in Tokyo.

  • Creator of VIBeID, a 100-person footstep vibration dataset
  • 5 journal papers, 5 conference papers and 4 patents in vibration sensing
  • Reviewer for IEEE TIM and IEEE TMC; ICASSP 2025 organising committee
  • Poster presenter at the Heidelberg Laureate Forum 2026
  • Also built GajGamini, ground-vibration sensing for elephant safety
