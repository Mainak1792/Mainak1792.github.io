Real
Floor recordings (two lab participants, 5 min each, 2 kHz), step detection, CWT, gait timing, the “is this the resident?” check, the JSON, and the Gemini calls.

  
Simulated
Earlier hours of the day, the 14-day history, and the five kinds of day. “Walking slower” re-times the real steps 18% further apart.

  
Simplified
In the browser, identity uses a small CWT fingerprint. On the real device, DeepStep (22 MB, ~0.8 s per step on a Raspberry Pi) does this job.
