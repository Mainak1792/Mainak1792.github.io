THE PRODUCT

One small sensor. Three kind outcomes.

小さなセンサー１つで、３つのやさしさ。

A daily note for the family

Each evening the family gets a short, warm update in plain words: how she walked, how she slept, anything new.

A gentle alert when it matters

A heavy impact followed by silence, or an unusually quiet day, becomes a clear request to check in, with a suggested action.

A friendly voice for her

Gemini speaks a short, cheerful message at home: “You walked well today.” Care that talks to her, not only about her.

PERSONAL, NOT AVERAGE

“Slower” means slower than her usual days.

Gemini compares today with the resident’s own last two weeks. Small changes in pace, rhythm or night-time walking become early, gentle signals, long before they become emergencies.

  • Walking speed and steadiness of rhythm
  • Night walks between midnight and 5 a.m.
  • Quiet hours with no movement at all
  • Visitors, recognised as “not the resident”

HOW IT WORKS

From a footstep to a kind word.

The hard signal work happens inside the home. Gemini does the human part.

01 · SENSE

Feel the floor

A geophone on the floor feels each step as a tiny vibration.

床の振動を感じる
02 · UNDERSTAND

Read each step at home

A small edge device finds steps, who is walking, and how.

家の中で一歩ずつ読み取る
03 · SUMMARISE

Send only numbers

About 300 bytes per walk leave the home. Never the raw signal.

数字だけを送る
04 · CARE

Gemini writes and speaks

Gemini compares with her usual days, chooses an action, and writes kindly.

Geminiがやさしく伝える

Explore the architecture

WHY GEMINI

The right job for each part.

Gemini is not asked to read raw vibration. A small, proven model at home does that. Gemini does what it is best at: judging change over time, choosing an action, and speaking to people with warmth.

  • Long-context reasoning over today plus a 14-day personal baseline
  • Function calling that plugs into any app: LINE, email, a care-home dashboard
  • Natural Japanese and English for families across borders
  • Text-to-speech for a friendly voice in the home
